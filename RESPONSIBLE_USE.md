# Responsible use

WhoFi is intended for educational and research use. The MIT License permits commercial use, but users should:

- respect privacy and obtain appropriate consent when deploying positioning systems;
- follow applicable laws and regulations concerning WiFi monitoring;
- assess the safety, security, and ethical effects of a deployment; and
- cite the project and the original WhoFi research when using it in academic work.

This guidance is not an additional license condition.
